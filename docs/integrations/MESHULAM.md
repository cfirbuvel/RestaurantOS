## 1. Overview
Meshulam is an Israeli payment clearing provider supporting Shva (שב"א) credit card rails, Bit, Apple Pay, and payment installment plans.
- **Capabilities:**
  - Tokenization of credit cards (J5 / token transactions)
  - Direct charging and pre-authorization
  - Refund processing
  - Inbound webhook confirmation
