## 1. Overview
Rivhit Online is an enterprise accounting and inventory management platform in Israel.
- **Features:** Document issuance (Invoices, Receipts, Credit Invoices) and journal ledger synchronization.
- **Documents Supported:** Tax Invoices (1), Receipts (2), Invoice-Receipts (3), Credit Invoices (4).
